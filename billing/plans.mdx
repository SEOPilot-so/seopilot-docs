## Founding price lock

If you subscribe during SEOPilot's founding period, your price is locked in for as long as your subscription stays active. You will never be automatically moved to a higher price tier.

<Warning>
  If you cancel your subscription and resubscribe later, you'll be placed on the then-current pricing at the time of resubscription. The founding rate cannot be reinstated after cancellation.
</Warning>

## How to subscribe

Monthly and annual checkout are both available at [seopilot.so](https://seopilot.so). Select the plan that works best for you, complete checkout, and SEOPilot will start crawling your site right away.

<Note>
  Billing is processed securely via Polar. You'll receive a receipt email after each payment. To view or download past invoices, go to Settings → Billing in your dashboard.
</Note>
