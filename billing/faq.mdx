---

Find answers to the most common billing and account questions below. For anything not covered here, email [support@seopilot.so](mailto:support@seopilot.so).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from Settings → Billing. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the current billing period, so you keep full access until that period ends. There are no cancellation fees, no questions asked.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does annual billing work?">
    When you choose the Annual plan, you're charged $190 once per year — which breaks down to $15.83 per month. You keep access for the full 12-month period even if you decide to cancel early. Annual billing saves you the equivalent of two months compared to paying monthly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my founding price if I cancel?">
    Your founding price is locked for as long as your subscription remains active. If you cancel and decide to resubscribe at a later date, you'll be placed on the current pricing at that time. The founding rate cannot be restored after a cancellation, so keep this in mind before pausing or stopping your plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get a receipt or invoice?">
    Receipts are emailed automatically to your billing address after each payment. You can also download past invoices at any time from Settings → Billing in your SEOPilot dashboard. If a receipt didn't arrive, check your spam folder or contact [support@seopilot.so](mailto:support@seopilot.so).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    SEOPilot accepts all major credit and debit cards, processed securely via Polar. Additional payment methods may be available at checkout depending on your location. SEOPilot does not store your card details directly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch from monthly to annual?">
    Yes. Go to Settings → Billing and click **Upgrade to Annual**. You'll be charged the annual rate at your next billing cycle. Upgrading locks in your current founding price at the annual rate if you're still within the founding period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my payment fails?">
    If a payment fails, SEOPilot will retry the charge automatically. You'll receive an email notification letting you know what happened and how to update your payment method. If the issue isn't resolved within a few days, your account may be paused until the payment succeeds. To avoid any interruption, make sure your card details are up to date in Settings → Billing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    There's no free trial for the full product, but you can try the free traffic estimate and keyword finder at [seopilot.so](https://seopilot.so) with no signup required. This gives you a sense of the keyword opportunities available for your site before you commit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
