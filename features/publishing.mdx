SEOPilot gives you two publishing modes: Autopilot and Review-First. Both deliver articles directly to your own domain — the only difference is how much control you want to exercise along the way.

## Autopilot mode

In Autopilot mode, SEOPilot handles everything from keyword research to publishing without requiring any action from you. Each day, a freshly drafted, SEO-structured article is automatically delivered to your site and goes live on schedule.

Autopilot is the right choice if you want SEO running in the background while you focus on everything else. There are no approvals to give, no queues to check, no articles to push. Just consistent, daily content hitting your domain — day after day.

## Review-First mode

In Review-First mode, every article is queued in your dashboard before it publishes. You get to read it, judge it, and decide what happens next.

From the review queue you can:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approve as-is">
    If the article looks great, approve it with one click and it publishes to your domain on the next scheduled slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit before publishing">
    Open the built-in editor to adjust the title, meta description, body copy, slug, or images — then approve the updated version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Skip the article">
    Not the right topic for this week? Skip it entirely. SEOPilot will continue with the next queued article without any gap in your schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Review-First is ideal for teams with brand guidelines to uphold, founders who want a final read before anything goes live, or anyone publishing in a regulated or sensitive industry.

## Switching between modes

You can switch between Autopilot and Review-First at any time from your dashboard settings. The change takes effect for the next queued article — any articles already in flight are handled according to the mode that was active when they were drafted.

## Autopilot vs. Review-First at a glance

| | **Autopilot** | **Review-First** |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup effort** | One-time setup, then nothing | One-time setup; periodic reviews |
| **Daily time required** | Zero | 5–10 minutes per article |
| **Risk of off-brand content** | Low (voice-matched, but unreviewed) | Minimal (you approve before publish) |
| **Best for** | Solo founders, hands-off operators | Teams, regulated industries, brand-sensitive sites |

## Where articles are published

Every article SEOPilot writes is delivered to your domain — not hosted on SEOPilot's servers. You own all the content and all the SEO authority that builds over time. SEOPilot never takes credit for your rankings.
