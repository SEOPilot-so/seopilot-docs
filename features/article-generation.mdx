SEOPilot writes one fresh article for your site every day. Each piece is drafted in your brand voice, structured for search engines, and ready to publish — no prompting, no briefing, no copy-pasting required.

## Brand-voice matching

Generic AI content has a telltale sameness to it. SEOPilot avoids that by learning how you actually write before drafting a single word.

When you connect your site URL, SEOPilot crawls your existing pages and builds a voice profile from your real content. Every article it generates is then run through a voice pass — adjusting tone, sentence structure, vocabulary, and phrasing until it reads like something you'd be proud to publish under your name.

For a deeper look at how this works, see [Brand Voice Detection](/features/brand-voice).

## The built-in editor

You don't need to export an article to a separate tool just to tweak a headline. SEOPilot includes a built-in editor that lets you adjust every element of the article before it goes live:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit the title and meta description">
    Update the SEO title and meta description directly inside SEOPilot to reflect any product-specific nuances or branding preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the URL slug">
    Customize the suggested slug to match your site's URL conventions or to include additional keywords.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revise the body content">
    Make inline edits anywhere in the article — add your own examples, swap out phrasing, or insert a personal section to strengthen the piece.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The article's auto-selected hero image is shown alongside the editor for reference. To use a different image, swap it in on your own site after the article is published or exported.

## Article quality

Before an article surfaces in your dashboard, SEOPilot runs a polish pass that checks for tone consistency, SEO structure, and factual coherence. The goal is to give you a draft that's close to publish-ready — not a rough outline that needs a full rewrite.

That said, you're always encouraged to review each article, especially when it touches product-specific claims, pricing comparisons, or technical details that require insider knowledge.

<Warning>
  AI-generated content can occasionally include inaccuracies. Always review articles that include statistics, pricing comparisons, or product-specific claims before publishing. A quick read-through protects your credibility and your readers' trust.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Articles that include your own examples, proprietary data, or genuine opinions tend to outperform purely AI-generated pieces in search. Use the built-in editor to add a personal section — even a short paragraph of your own perspective can meaningfully improve rankings and reader engagement.
</Tip>
