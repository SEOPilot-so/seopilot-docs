## Get started in three steps

You don't need an SEO background, a content team, or any special technical setup. Here's all it takes to get SEOPilot working for your site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste your URL">
    Head to [seopilot.so](https://seopilot.so) and enter your site's URL. SEOPilot crawls your pages, reads your existing content, and builds a picture of your brand voice and topical authority — no signup required to see your first results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your keywords">
    Within about 60 seconds, SEOPilot surfaces 7 high-intent keyword opportunities matched to your domain's current authority level. These aren't generic suggestions — they're realistic targets your site can actually rank for. Swap out any you don't love before moving on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your first article">
    Choose Autopilot mode to let SEOPilot publish immediately, or turn on Review-First if you'd like to read and approve each article before it goes live. Either way, your first SEO-optimized article lands on your domain within 24 hours of completing setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  SEOPilot publishes articles to YOUR domain — all SEO credit stays with you. Your domain's authority grows with every article, not ours.
</Note>
