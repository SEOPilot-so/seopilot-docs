SEOPilot is an AI-powered SEO automation platform built for founders, indie hackers, and small teams who want organic traffic without hiring an SEO agency or content team. You connect your site, and SEOPilot takes it from there — crawling your content, learning your brand voice, researching the best keywords for your domain every week, and publishing a fresh, optimized article to your site every day. It's the SEO engine your business always needed, running quietly in the background while you focus on building.

## The SEO problem SEOPilot solves

SEO is one of the highest-leverage growth channels for any online business — but it's also the first thing that gets deprioritized when you're a small team. Product ships, sales calls stack up, and the content calendar stays empty. Even founders who *want* to invest in SEO run into the same walls:

- **SEO keeps losing to product and sales work.** It's rarely urgent, so it's always last. Weeks turn into months without a single article published.
- **Publishing stalls with one-person teams.** Writing high-quality, keyword-optimized content takes hours per article. Doing it consistently — every day — is simply not realistic without a dedicated hire.
- **Low-authority domains struggle with generic, high-volume keywords.** Targeting the same terms as established players means ranking on page 10, if at all. You need keywords matched to where your domain actually stands today.

SEOPilot removes every one of these friction points. There are no briefs to write, no keyword spreadsheets to maintain, no drafts to commission. The system runs itself.

## What SEOPilot does

SEOPilot operates on a simple, repeating cycle that runs from the moment you connect your site:

1. **Weekly keyword research.** Every week, SEOPilot researches 7 high-intent keywords specifically chosen for your domain's current authority level and niche. These are terms real people search for, with realistic ranking potential for a site like yours.
2. **Daily AI article drafting.** SEOPilot writes a fully structured, SEO-optimized article for each keyword — in your brand's voice, using your existing content as a reference. Articles include meta titles, H2/H3 headings, FAQ sections, and suggested internal links.
3. **Automatic publishing to your domain.** Each article is delivered directly to your site via webhook, or downloaded as MDX/CSV export. SEOPilot never hosts the content — your domain gets all the traffic and all the SEO credit.
