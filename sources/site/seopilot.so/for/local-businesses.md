## Common objections

### We rely more on referrals than content

Referrals and search are complementary. Local SEO helps capture the demand that exists when people ask for recommendations and then verify options online.

### We do not have time to manage another tool

The workflow is designed to reduce busywork, not add another dashboard that requires daily attention.

## FAQ

### Is this only for multi-location businesses?

No. Single-location businesses can benefit when they consistently publish around the services and questions their local customers actually search.

### Does this replace local listings optimization?

No. It supports broader local discovery by adding useful content alongside the usual listings and profile work.
