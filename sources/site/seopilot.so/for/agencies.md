## Common objections

### Our clients need custom strategy

The workflow does not remove strategy. It removes repetitive production work so strategists can spend more time on judgment and less on formatting and chasing briefs.

### We cannot risk generic output

Persona-specific positioning, review-first workflows, and voice-aware drafting keep the process useful without collapsing into interchangeable content.

## FAQ

### Can agencies still keep a review step?

Yes. The product messaging already supports review-first publishing rather than forcing full autopilot.

### Is this only for small agencies?

No. The strongest fit is any agency that needs more content production leverage without adding the same amount of labor.
