## Common objections

### We already have collection pages

Collection pages capture only a slice of intent. Supporting editorial content helps the site rank for the questions and comparisons buyers search before they are ready to browse listings.

### Our team is focused on paid acquisition

That is common. SEO works best here as a compounding complement, especially when the content workflow is operationally light enough to sustain.

## FAQ

### Is this useful if we sell a narrow catalog?

Yes. Narrow catalogs often benefit even more from supporting content because every relevant topic can feed a focused set of products.

### Does this replace product-page SEO?

No. It complements it by covering the research and comparison intent that product pages usually miss.
